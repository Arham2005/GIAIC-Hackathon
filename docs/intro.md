# Welcome to Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics

## 📚 About This Book

Welcome to your comprehensive guide to **Physical AI and Humanoid Robotics**! This book explores the exciting intersection of artificial intelligence and robotics, focusing on creating intelligent machines that can perceive, think, and act in the physical world.

## 🎯 What You'll Learn

This book covers the essential topics you need to master Physical AI:

### Foundations
- Understanding Physical AI concepts and principles
- Robotics fundamentals and mechanical systems
- Sensors, actuators, and hardware components

### Core Techniques
- Kinematics and dynamics of robot motion
- Machine learning for robotic applications
- Control systems and trajectory planning

### Advanced Topics
- Humanoid robot design and bipedal locomotion
- Neural networks for robot control
- Real-world deployment and safety considerations

## 👥 Who This Book Is For

- **Students** learning robotics and AI
- **Engineers** building robotic systems
- **Researchers** exploring Physical AI
- **Enthusiasts** curious about intelligent machines
